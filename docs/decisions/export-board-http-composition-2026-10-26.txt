lookup, card mutations and recovery preview/confirmation routes. H01 retains grant

components in the executable. No component handoff establishes product activation.

authority, route, precondition and delivery obligations.

inventory and raw protected BLOB union. Compact or refused byte facts preserve
evidence without independently pinning bytes. Another export's extra pins protect

member/byte/JSON/depth limits and COMPLETE remain the accepted contract. Validation

S02 lookup path supplies this distinction; there is no adapter-side ledger query.
Recovery preview is read-only and grants no authority;
confirmation retains S03's original immutable preview, current rights, exact
revision and receipt-first rules. The accepted NO_CHANGE policy remains binding.

Executable composition, offline validation/restore, CLI/browser delivery and native
