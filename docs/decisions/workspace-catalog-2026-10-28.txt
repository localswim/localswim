M01.06 truthful catalog projection and bounded refresh
2026-10-28

GET /api/v1/workspace/catalog projects explicit workspace membership through its
authenticated browser or automation channel. Each member's configured read grant
and all board facts are checked in one actual read-only SQL snapshot. Catalog reads
do not change priorities, approval, history, board state or storage schemas.

Facts and authority
- Ready for Review counts exclude archived cards; known zero is a valid value.
  Blocked and Needs Terry remain not_configured/null until the workflow milestone.
  Unknown and failed probes never become a current zero.
- Entries include observation/freshness, revision, availability and honest retained
  committed-card activity/coverage. Archived edits and compact operation provenance
  contribute; file times, labels and recovery-expiry housekeeping do not. Future
  timestamps remain ordered as stored; elapsed age is clamped at zero.
- Failed or unfinished current probes return opaque unknown facts without prior
  identity, epoch or counts. Here never freshness means no usable authorized
  observation for that response. Completed cached facts do not grant current access.
- In-flight coalescing tracks joining requests. A result whose snapshot began
  before a later join is discarded and re-read under the same original one-second
  job context and slot. Every recipient is covered by the snapshot's authority;
  identity replacement or permission revocation cannot expose an older job's facts
  to a newly joining poll. Completed jobs are removed atomically with joining.
- Browser and automation work remain partitioned. No cross-board atomic snapshot
  or continuously current observation is claimed.

Scheduling and ordering
Activation and projection share at most four outstanding jobs. FIFO pending data
and alternating queues prevent member starvation without creating a goroutine per
waiting member. Native work, SQL and drain occur outside bookkeeping locks. A hung
call retains its slot until it actually returns; later polls do not duplicate it.
Each attempt uses one second and each catalog response waits at most two seconds
before publishing independent results/unknowns. This does not bound arbitrary
native disk/driver execution. The later browser shell owns five-second visible
polling, focus/retry/local-commit triggers and one in-flight poll per window.

Other ordering is committed activity descending, supported fresh known attention on
exact activity ties, then full Unicode case-folded label and exact project/board
keys. Unknown activity has no invented score. Time passing alone does not reorder.
Unicode 17 folding uses the pinned Go tables plus 298 generated full-fold exceptions,
with no normalization or new module dependency. The helper was compared exhaustively
with the pinned Python Unicode 17 oracle over all 1,112,064 valid scalars; multi-rune
expansions/ties and deterministic generation are separately checked.

Read-only projection joins Runtime admission/drain. Its current permission, revision,
counts and provenance share one transaction. New durable facts or ephemeral grants
are not created. The generated catalog DTOs, schemas and browser validators remain
Go-owned and strict about nullability, supported categories and observation state.

Evidence boundary
Native Go/HTTP tests exercise real mutations, read revocation, identity replacement,
one hung member among healthy members, four-job limits, fairness, late joins and
deadline pressure. Archived/pruned/future provenance is a separately labeled,
schema-validated technical read fixture, not a new archive command or SQL customer
seed path. Real executable browser observations supplement the backend checks.
No ordinary sidebar, pin controls, full joined M01 qualification, import/cutover or
power-loss assurance is claimed. Private runtime data and reports remain private.
