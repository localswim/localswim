## Browser differences and handoff

- **Board-wide Newest comments (10, 18).** The two latest comments appear in server order with links to their source cards. The focused drawer keeps its card-scoped preview. [Board preview](../../web/src/newest-comments.ts) · [Focused preview](../../web/src/comments.ts) · [REST contract](../openapi.yaml)
- **Explicit healthy-board Refresh (03).** The prior shell exposed Refresh on the selected board and workspace, subject to an active write guard. T8 provided automatic refresh, a retry after failure and focused inspection actions. Reload rereads the route. T9 exposes Refresh on the board and in Workspace. Its status reports the catalog or authoritative board read. Sidebar, publishing and drawer reads have their own status. Refresh preserves in-progress drafts and keeps the existing mutation fences. [Prior Refresh handler](https://github.com/localswim/localswim/blob/18a095f/web/app.mjs#L5675) · [Candidate app wiring](../../web/src/main.ts)
- **Selected-board durable health (34).** The prior browser differentiated a diverged snapshot, missing/unavailable snapshot and startup failure, with explicit board identity on the startup diagnostic. T8 already detected health changes and fenced the board, but cleared the current display on a catalog-confirmed unhealthy state. T9 restores the sanitized distinction and last confirmed read-only display where a valid prior view exists. Startup quarantine recovery remains an explicit CLI/API operation. [Prior health handling](https://github.com/localswim/localswim/blob/18a095f/web/app.mjs#L3613) · [Candidate board state](../../web/src/board-state.ts) · [REST contract](../openapi.yaml)
- **Pending lane faces (25).** The prior display projected pending title and priority edits, move destinations and an unnumbered new card while preserving confirmed membership for lane actions. T8 retains mutation status and original-request recovery in its controls. T9 restores the lane-face projection for those four submitted actions. Results retire when a new authoritative snapshot replaces the starting revision or the action fails. [Prior projection](https://github.com/localswim/localswim/blob/18a095f/web/app.mjs#L4386) · [Candidate board renderer](../../web/src/board-view.ts)
- **Exhaustive archive browsing (21).** The former Archived cards disclosure is available through Find cards with Archived cards selected. Load more follows the archive cursor and preserves keyboard access to appended rows. [Find cards](../../web/src/search.ts) · [REST contract](../openapi.yaml)
- **V1 visual and interaction reference.** Header placement, sidebar search, card and lane density, color and responsive layout belong to the side-by-side design review. The Architect's `0514-v1-ux-inventory.md` records a read-only V1 preview from 2026-11-12.
