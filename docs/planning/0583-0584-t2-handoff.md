## Configured styles

The renderer applies the selected board's stored stylesheet in its shadow
root. The baseline stylesheet is served locally under the existing CSP.
The live synthetic Alpha style set a 4 px purple card outline. The workspace
kept its default colors, and Beta returned to zero adopted sheets and its
default lane color after selection. The style was cleared from the fixture.

isolates selector matching. Constructable stylesheets ignore `@import`.

legacy configured-selector set. The supported hooks include `.card` and
`.lane` within the board.
