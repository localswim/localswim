F2 (material): Buffer gives the final private allocation to its source Reader.

- internal/board/attachments/upload.go:21 calls io.ReadFull(r, b). A source Reader
  can retain b and change the Upload after Buffer returns and after Add verifies
  its SHA-256. The output-reader correction does not seal this input callback.
- A source retaining its supplied destination violates the normal io.Reader
  convention, just as F1's hostile Writer violated io.Writer's convention. The
  required opaque immutable Upload boundary must not expose its final storage to
  either callback direction.
- Independent deterministic reproduction retained the source destination, then
  changed "good" to "evil" during current credential verification. Reusing a
  healthy stored digest falsely fenced the store as persistent integrity damage.
  The reproduced input source hash was stable throughout the failing test.

Required correction: read through bounded temporary scratch storage and copy
into the final private allocation. Keep the one-file buffer bound, avoid a second
whole-file allocation, and independently verify retained-source mutation before
and during Add for new and reused digests.
