B02 LINKS, HANDOFF AND APPROVAL - INDEPENDENT REVIEW
2026-10-26 | Revision 1

Verdict: PASS for the bounded B02 workflow component and shared snapshot facade.
No unresolved finding remains in this scope. Primary integration and owner
acceptance remain separate gates.

Admission and graph behavior
- Independently resolved board, incarnation and owner-card routes are captured
  by value and compared with frozen typed intent. Link targets remain peers or
  prerequisites in the same board incarnation; they do not supply route authority.
- Real G01 credential/current-right checks compose with the real S01/S02 writer.
  Frozen callbacks do not cache grants. Removed rights, revoked/replaced grants,
  automation approval and native-owner ordinary workflow authority are denied.
- Self-links and new active blocking cycles refuse; informational cycles are
  permitted. Active typed pairs are unique. Duplicate adds yield
  LINK_ALREADY_EXISTS; inactive/noncurrent removals yield NOT_FOUND. Wrong-owner
  removal refuses. Removal retains the immutable historical link identity, while
  re-adding a removed pair allocates a new identity.
- Informational and removed links never gate start. Direct blocking prerequisites
  still require nonarchived Completed cards with current human approval under
  B01's existing admission. No priority propagation or assignment policy is added.

Approval and transaction behavior
- Handoff admits only In Progress -> Ready for Review with its current right; it
  creates neither an approval fact nor approval authority. Approval admits only
  Ready for Review -> Completed for a current human with approve authority.
- Approval records the exact reviewed pre-approval card and board revisions and
  current policy version, advances the card once and installs the new approval.
  Other real card mutations clear current approval and retain historical facts.
  Completed remains immutable. Handoff/approval preserve the archived flag;
  archived prerequisites do not satisfy start admission.
- Policy version 1 remains frozen by C01 and schema 3. Unsupported policy input
  and physical policy changes are refused; no policy migration is introduced.
- Exact board/card revisions, current admission, full prior snapshots, prior
  comment/link/attachment/approval references and byte pins are preserved in the
  serialized transaction before current graph references change. Snapshot origin,
  actor and time come from unique retained COMMITTED metadata, including compact
  source provenance. Missing/ambiguous provenance fails closed and rolls back.
- The new cards snapshot facade delegates to the existing private B01 core. It
  adds scope/state validation without duplicating snapshot or pinning logic.
- Each success changes only the owner card once and produces one board revision,
  attributable activity event and committed receipt. Terminal refusals preserve
  domain state, approval, graph, history and counters. Exact retained refusal
  replay cannot later mutate the graph. Counter limits and post-effect rollback
  behavior were checked through the actual providers.
