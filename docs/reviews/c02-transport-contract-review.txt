Assessment and limits

full typed results, compact null/removal rules and refusal-code binding are checked
structurally. Matching text/hash or echoed opId supplies no terminal authenticity.

lookup/possible-submission uncertainty cannot become proof of original noncommit.

Secret transport bodies reject ordinary JSON marshaling and public rendering;
default/debug formatting is redacted. Explicit MarshalEphemeral remains an opt-in
for validated ephemeral transport only. Deliberate extraction/conversion and unsafe
caller-written diagnostic text remain outside those presentation guards.

required. This PASS does not qualify auth, HTTP/multipart, storage, ZIP/native IO,
