Outcome
Three reproducible storage validation defects require correction. The author
created none of this review or its private supplemental tests. Scope is schema,
open and physical connection ownership; this review grants no product recovery,
admission, retention, archive or durability qualification.

R1 High - Legal extra schema objects escape the exact-layout audit
- internal/store/schema/schema.go:146 excludes names with LIKE 'sqlite_%'.
  The underscore is a wildcard. A legal CREATE TABLE sqliteXunexpected(x INTEGER)
  succeeds, and Validate accepts the resulting unsupported layout.
- Correct the reserved-prefix filter to match the literal sqlite_ prefix, or
  inventory all supported objects explicitly. Add a legal lookalike-object
  regression and verify failed Existing leaves database bytes unchanged.
- Private TestReviewHiddenLayoutRejected reproduces the acceptance. This concerns
  schema identity, not hostile same-process isolation or later domain admission.

R2 High - A committed attachment's byte fact can disagree with its protected bytes
- internal/store/schema/ddl.go:246 validates a pin against Attachment, but not
  against its operation's byte_fact. schema.go:238 performs the same incomplete
  audit; its global digest-length check does not compare different digests.
- A full COMMITTED attachment.add with byte_fact digest A and an operation pin
  protecting Attachment/BLOB digest B passes both insert and Validate. Both facts
  can have length zero, and B's stored bytes can have the correct SHA-256.
- Bind that operation's digest/length fact to its full committed attachment pin
  in transaction-completeness validation and appropriate insert constraints.
  Add a mismatching-digest regression. Preserve refused/compact facts' freedom
  from BLOB FKs or fabricated entities; no JSON canonical decoder is requested.
- Private TestReviewOperationByteFactPinMismatchRejected reproduces acceptance.
  This violates D02 archive-recovery-schema.txt:193-213's relational byte mapping.

R3 Medium - Full historical references can belong to another card
- internal/store/schema/ddl.go:139-144 keys revision_reference to a snapshot and
  entity existence, omitting the entity's card ownership. The consistency audit
  at schema.go:229 checks tombstone/full availability but not ownership.
- A full snapshot of card A can reference card B's full comment; insert and
  Validate both succeed. Current references correctly include ownership in FKs.
- Bind historical reference card_id to entity.card_id, including retained
  tombstones, or equivalently audit it. Cover comments, links, attachments and
  approvals; keep valid same-card full and removed-history cases passing.
- Private TestReviewCrossCardHistoryRejected reproduces the full-comment case.
