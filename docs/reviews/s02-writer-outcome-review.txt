Transaction and authority assessment

only the SELECT seam; Apply stages its domain effects within the same transaction.
The writer owns the board increment, checks affected-card revision results,
validates exact retained results, inserts full receipts and attachment operation
pins, audits storage consistency, and explicitly commits. SQL gates reject stacked
statements and transaction control. Missing or malformed/corrupt/source evidence
never becomes a NOT_COMMITTED claim or permission to replay.

Callback and context failures trigger independent bounded rollback and lease
cleanup. Unknown rollback/reconciliation fences writes. Explicit close and validated
Existing reopen are required to resume; no retry or unfence shortcut is supplied.
Authority is rechecked from current data for retained submit, lookup and fresh
post-commit reconciliation, including an operation that revoked its own authority.
