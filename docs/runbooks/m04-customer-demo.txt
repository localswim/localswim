M04 NORMAL CUSTOMER REVIEW
2026-10-30 | Fresh synthetic data, actual local executable and browser

Use docs/runbooks/m04-start-use-recovery.txt to verify the executable, start a new
workspace and register Harbor / Dispatch and Harbor / Research. Use the printed
localhost address. Do not repeat registration on restart.

1. Select Dispatch, read current board and create a P3 parent and child. Open the
   parent's details, choose Create child, enter a title and Save. Navigate from
   parent to child and back. Read the exact UUIDs with the selected real CLI:

     $selection = @('--workspace', $workspace, '--project', 'harbor', '--board', 'dispatch')
     & $exe @selection --json board read

2. Create another parent. Open the child, choose Set parent, deliberately select
   that parent and Save. Read current hierarchy, then choose explicit clear and
   Save. Confirm the child is now a root. Priority, lane, owner and approval do
   not propagate. Completed children are read-only; archived or Completed cards
   can be parent targets. Parentage grants no rights and does not waive gates.
3. Create a card on Research too. Compare its full board/card identity to the
   Dispatch card; equal display tickets do not identify the same card. Return to
   Dispatch. Find cards with literal text, Apply filters and open a result. Use
   Tab and Enter/Space; close details and observe the available focus return.
4. Confirm Archive on an unfinished card. Reapply the search: Active excludes it.
   Select Archived or All, find the same card and confirm Unarchive. Read current
   board and reapply Active. Completed remains read-only.
5. Stop the service with Ctrl+C and wait for exit. Start it again with
   & $exe serve --workspace $workspace. It uses the saved port. Reconnect, read
   current board and inspect the committed hierarchy and card facts.

Keep private drafts and UNKNOWN originals separate from current reads. A normal
refresh or restart does not replay or settle an original. This demonstration does
not inject faults. Qualification separately records controlled uncertainty, bounds,
upgrade and other evidence; review of those reports is not a repeat of those tests.
