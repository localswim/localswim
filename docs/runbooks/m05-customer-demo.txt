M05 CUSTOMER DEMONSTRATION
2026-11-04 | Normal synthetic walkthrough; record what is actually observed

Use docs/runbooks/m05-start-use-recovery.txt to verify the M05 executable and
create a fresh private synthetic workspace. Keep the service running. Choose
Harbor / Dispatch and wait for Read current board to become Current. Do not use
an administrative or everyday board. This is a proposed demonstration, not a
claim that the demonstration has already passed.

1. Create a prerequisite, a parent and at least four other peer cards with
   distinctive synthetic titles.
   Choose priority and owner explicitly; observe their lane and save each once.
   Open the parent and Create child. In that editor choose priority and owner,
   and add a Depends on relationship to the existing prerequisite before saving.
   Observe the child and its parent and relationship after current reads: creation,
   parent and relationship were submitted together. Attempt Start work while the
   prerequisite is unfinished; observe its gate without treating it as approval.
2. Open the child and add Informs, Related to, References and Duplicates using
   suitable distinct existing peers. Observe the outgoing labels and navigate to
   a peer to inspect its derived incoming label and actual owner. To change an
   incoming fact, deliberately open its owner; do not mutate the peer indirectly.
3. Return to the child and use Replace owned relationships. Remove one owned
   relationship and save; inspect the retained owned facts and incoming facts.
   Observe that parent, priority, owner and lane do not automatically change.
   Explore reparent/clear through the explicit controls and restore a parent if
   desired. Read current hierarchy and relationships whenever marked stale.
4. Find the child by its synthetic title. Archive an unfinished card through its
   confirmation; select Archived or All, find it and confirm Unarchive. Read
   current board. Use Tab and Enter/Space for named controls, then close details
   and observe focus return. Multiline Enter and dirty Escape retain their stated
   editor behavior; do not submit twice.
5. Switch to Harbor / Research and create a separate synthetic card. Confirm the
   selected board and exact identity; matching ticket numbers do not identify the
   same card. Switch back to Dispatch and read current facts. In the second
   PowerShell window run:

     $selection = @('--workspace', $workspace, '--project', 'harbor', '--board', 'dispatch')
     & $exe @selection --json board read
     & $exe @selection card relationships 'CHILD-UUID-FROM-THAT-READ'

   Inspect the actual owner and peer identities and the captured revision. Replace
   the placeholder with the returned UUID, not its displayed ticket.
6. Stop the foreground service with Ctrl+C and wait for exit. Reopen with
   & $exe serve --workspace $workspace. Refresh boards, reconnect if needed and
   read Dispatch again; observe the persisted cards, hierarchy and relationships.
   Leave unresolved originals intact. If any outcome is UNKNOWN, use the recovery
   guide and deliberate original lookup; never replay merely to finish the demo.

Record each observed result and any mismatch privately, with sanitized evidence.
Stop on a defect and retain the failed attempt. This normal walkthrough does not
replace the separately recorded qualification, offline upgrade, restore, fault,
concurrency or physical power-loss evidence. The kit's manifest and report bind
its exact binary and source. If the kit exceeds a 10 MiB attachment limit, use
its supplied local path and manifest.
