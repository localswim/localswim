M08 BROWSER ATTENTION CATEGORIES
2026-11-05 | M08.04 ordinary browser guide

Open the selected board and choose Edit workflow. Attention categories share
that editor's complete candidate, private draft, explicit preview and Save.
Existing lane IDs and roles stay visible. Category choices use those IDs.

Category meanings
- Blocked waits on an external condition neither Terry nor Bot can advance.
- Needs Terry requires a specific input or action to finish the work.
- Ready for Review awaits Terry's review or approval.

Configuration
- An untouched workflow uses implicit assignments. Blocked and Needs Terry
  are unconfigured. The protected review lane supplies Ready for Review.
- Use explicit Attention assignments records a deliberate complete assignment,
  even if its effective mapping matches the implicit mapping.
- Each waiting lane offers Not configured, Blocked and Needs Terry. Choosing
  a category removes that lane from the other waiting category.
- The review lane offers Not configured and Ready for Review. Disabling this
  measure preserves the protected review lane and its approval behavior.
- Disable all Attention categories records three explicit empty sets.
- Restore implicit Attention assignments removes the optional attention field.
- In new-board creation, changing a lane role refreshes category controls.
  Explicit assignments that become invalid stay visible until cleared or the
  role is restored. Validation refuses them.
- A lane removal also removes its category membership in the same candidate.
  The authoritative preview still checks occupants and compatibility.

Review and save
Preview workflow is read-only. Its Attention section shows current/candidate
sources, stable lane IDs and roles, and current/candidate counts. Not configured
means the mapping is empty. A configured category with no unarchived current
cards displays 0. Candidate counts estimate the proposed mapping over the
current committed cards. Review the complete preview, then choose Save workflow.
Any later input change requires another preview.

Labels, transitions, ceilings and category assignments can change together in
one candidate and one policy version. Category edits preserve card state and
human approvals. A category is selected deliberately through normal lane use.

Private input and uncertain results
Refusal and private-storage failures preserve input. Stale context requires a
fresh board read and deliberate new editor. Cancel prompts before discarding
changed input. Keyboard and composition handling match the workflow editor.
An uncertain save retains the exact original and candidate. Reloading or opening
another window preserves the private original without replay or automatic
lookup. Use Look up original outcome deliberately to reconcile it.
