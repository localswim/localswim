RECOVER INTERRUPTED NOTICES IN THE BROWSER

Open the ordinary local board or select its member in the workspace. Open
Notices and choose Read status. Verify the board, incarnation, saved target
and checkpoint. A guarded monitor may display the original pending delivery
ID, lower event, upper event and count.

1. Choose Recover when the selected target is guarded or failed. Recover
   settles eligible saved work. It does not start a monitor. While waiting,
   the panel keeps the last read status visible.
2. Check the returned status. Stopped means the monitor has settled. When
   the result remains guarded, check its reason and the retained pending
   evidence. UNKNOWN means acceptance could not be established. A guarded
   unavailable result can retain a gap and the previous checkpoint.
3. Use Look up receipt with the saved target and original delivery ID to
   check for positive proof. An accepted receipt shows its original
   acceptance time and cuts. Read inbox can also show the retained receipt.
4. When recovery settles to Stopped, choose Read status, then Start if you
   want normal monitoring and catch-up from the proved checkpoint.

Each Recover uses the generation from a deliberate Read status. A stale
generation or busy monitor requires a fresh status read before any further
control. If a response is lost, the outcome is UNKNOWN. Read status and
inspect the exact receipt before choosing another action. The browser sends
one request per action. Closing Notices, switching boards or reconnecting
clears the view and cancels its request. The service may already have
admitted the action.

The panel supports keyboard navigation. Close notices returns focus to its
opener. Notice recovery leaves card drafts alone. See m12-notices-browser.txt
for ordinary monitoring and localpost-development.txt for test diagnostics.
