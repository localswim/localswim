NOTICES: RELOAD AND FUTURE TARGETS

Open the selected board and choose Notices, then Read status. The displayed
control generation and configuration revision bind the next deliberate action.
A control uses those exact values once. Read status again before another action.

Reload cooperatively replaces a running monitor while retaining its board,
service, future target and configuration revision. Reload on a stopped or guarded
monitor does not start it or recover pending work.

To change future observations, enter a canonical lowercase UUIDv4 in Future
target ID and choose Retarget. The input accepts exactly 36 characters without
spaces. A target names a partition of this board's private local inbox; it is
not another board, a URL, filesystem path or external destination. External
Codex delivery is outside this feature.

Future target and configuration revision describe future observations. Original
pending and Last outcome show their own target, delivery ID and event cuts.
An observation already open before Retarget keeps its original target. Retarget
does not recover or resend uncertain work. A stopped or guarded monitor remains
stopped or guarded. Use deliberate Recover or Start only after reading status
and considering the original evidence.

Read inbox lists retained local receipts across targets. For exact proof, enter
the original Target ID and Delivery ID, read status, then choose Look up receipt.
An accepted receipt shows its original acceptance time and cuts. Missing proof
means UNKNOWN; it cannot establish that nothing happened. Local acceptance does
not assert delivery outside the local inbox.

Partial drain means the original operation is incomplete. The owner keeps its
reservation while admitted work finishes. Read status to observe eventual state.
A stale or busy refusal requires new status and a new deliberate action. A lost,
timed-out or exact unknown reply displays UNKNOWN: the request may have taken
effect. Original response evidence remains separate from later status. Do not
repeat the action solely because its result was unavailable.

Target and receipt-input drafts stay in this window's memory for their board
and incarnation. Notice requests do not submit or clear card-editor drafts.
Closing Notices returns focus to its opener. Leaving the view, reconnecting or
changing selection invalidates displayed private evidence and outstanding replies;
read status deliberately after reopening. No background control refresh or replay
occurs. All product requests keep the selected service's authentication and CSP.
