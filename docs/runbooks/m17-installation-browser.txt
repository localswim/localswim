Inspect the supported installation without running it

Open the ordinary local board, or select the exact board in the workspace.
Choose Recipient policy, then Inspect installation deliberately. The action
reads the current engine generation and requests one installation inspection.
Opening the panel, reading recipient status, reconnecting or navigating does
not inspect automatically. Inspection does not save a recipient or run Codex.

Interpret the result
- Candidate present; no command was run: current installation metadata passed
  the supported inspection policy. This is not an executable version, interface,
  capability, target permission, delivery or future launch-readiness result.
- No candidate in the supported installation location: that bounded location
  or its qualified executable was absent. Another installation may exist.
- Qualification not established: the supported inspection policy could not
  qualify this installation. This does not declare it unsafe or unusable.
  Inspection could not complete means required evidence was unavailable or the
  inspection deadline elapsed; a definite policy refusal is labeled separately.

While an inspection is pending, duplicate inspection and recipient-save actions
are disabled. Your recipient draft, saved policy, unresolved original save and
card editor remain separate. Inspection cannot settle or replay a recipient save.
It also works before a recipient has been configured and adds no delivery right.

Close recipient policy or Escape cancels waiting and returns focus to its opener.
Navigation, an authority change or a host-generation change clears the displayed
observation. A late response cannot paint another board, a reopened panel or
another window. Native cleanup can continue under the service's ownership after
the browser stops waiting; closing the panel does not prove that cleanup ended.

On refused or unavailable inspection, retain your input and inspect again only
as a new deliberate action if needed. No automatic retry, install, command, Stop,
Copy, activation or delivery action is provided. The same-origin selected-board
service remains authoritative; private paths, hashes and signer details are not
displayed. The existing M16 recipient and M12-M15 notice controls keep their own
scope, original evidence and recovery rules.
