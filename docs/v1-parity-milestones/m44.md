## Work

Support pointer and keyboard resize and collapse. Keep the chosen expanded width per tab across reload, clamp to the available viewport and retain the fixed count alignment when the sidebar is expanded.

## Demo and acceptance

Resize and collapse in one tab, leave another at its default, then reload both and reopen the first sidebar at its saved width.
