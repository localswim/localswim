## Work

Provide a supported explicit native recovery path using a verified snapshot or backup. Establish identity from trusted local state or an explicitly verified recovery input, validate before replacing, and avoid a second writer. Preserve a repairable failure on refusal. Where identity or revision continuity cannot be established, expose explicit recovery as a fresh board identity.

## Demo and acceptance

Recover the damaged board from a known backup while the other board remains useful. Refuse a mismatched backup and show both durable state and restored board identity.
