## Work

Provide a supported explicit native recovery path using a verified snapshot or backup. Establish identity from trusted local state or an explicitly verified recovery input, validate before replacing, and avoid a second writer. Preserve a repairable failure on refusal. Where identity or revision continuity cannot be established, expose explicit recovery as a fresh board identity.

## Demo and acceptance

Recover the damaged board from a known backup while the other board remains useful. Refuse a mismatched backup and show both durable state and restored board identity.

## Implemented interface

- `board recover --mode same_id BOARD_ID FILE|-` reads the live failure diagnostic
  and conditionally admits a fully verified native JSON snapshot or ZIP backup.
- `board recover --mode fresh BOARD_ID FILE|-` explicitly creates a new identity,
  preserves exact prose and global users, and retains the failure.
- Both modes disable publishing and audit the reset.
- Same-ID recovery preserves the board's catalog entry as its startup diagnostic
  gives way to the recovered board for readers with access.
- `POST /recoveries/{board_id}?mode=same_id|fresh` accepts the verification ETag.
  Refused or stale inputs remain repairable. An uncertain reply requires inspection.
