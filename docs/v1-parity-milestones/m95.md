## Work

Retain the minimum trusted local identity, authorization context and revision floor needed to recover a board after restart. Use existing committed metadata where possible. Report what can be recovered under the same ID and where only fresh-board recovery is safe. Refuse identity collisions with healthy boards.

## Demo and acceptance

Stop the service, damage one snapshot, and restart. Inspect the trustworthy recovery reference for that board and show a damaged or missing reference cannot authorize same-ID recovery.
