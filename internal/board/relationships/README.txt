M05 relationship transaction helper

Prepare runs after the command's normal current authority, identity, revision and
Completed admission in the sole writer snapshot. It returns a short-lived Plan;
never cache or apply a plan across transactions. There is no new command/event.

Replacement conserves exact active owned members without re-admitting historical
targets, deactivates omissions, and admits only new members against the final
proposed graph. Related reverse ownership is never transferred. Unchanged legacy
cycles/external records remain evidence; new blocking/duplicate edges cannot
close cycles. Other kinds and hierarchy are independent graphs.

Apply runs after the caller captures its source's prior snapshot. It writes new
entity/link/current-reference facts and removes only omitted source-owned current
references. It owns no card revision, approval, activity, result or original ledger;
cards/workflow and the existing writer retain those responsibilities.

Transaction integration proof lives in workflow/relationships_windows_test.go.
Those synthetic native tests require a fresh explicit private S01_TEST_ROOT and
the actual Windows account for native path leases/ACL validation. They do not
establish browser/CLI/restore qualification or physical power-loss safety.
