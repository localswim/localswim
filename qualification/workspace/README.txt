using an explicit unused numeric-loopback port. Reopen that same workspace without
--port to retain its configured port and registered identities. Selection is always
explicit; /board/PROJECT/BOARD never chooses a neighboring or remembered fallback.

local service and Read current board before opening new work on an expired board
channel. UNKNOWN originals require deliberate inspection in their original context;
navigation, reconnect and refresh never authorize resubmission.
